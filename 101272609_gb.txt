George Brown College

George Brown College is a public college in Toronto.
It has different programs for students.
I am studying Computer Programming and Analysis.

The college offers programs in business, technology,
health sciences, and other areas.
In my program, we study programming, databases,
and software development.

We have assignments and projects in our courses.
Some projects require us to work with other students.
I think group projects are useful because we can
learn how to share work and solve problems together.
