George Brown Polytechnic

George Brown Polytechnic is a public post-secondary institution in Toronto, Ontario.
It offers programs in technology, business, health sciences, design, hospitality,
construction, and many other areas.

I am studying Computer Programming and Analysis.
My program includes software development, databases, web development,
systems analysis, and practical programming projects.

One aspect I value about George Brown is the focus on practical learning.
Many courses require students to build applications, work with development tools,
and solve problems that are similar to real workplace situations.

Group projects also help students practice teamwork, communication,
version control, and task coordination. These skills are important
because software development is usually a collaborative process.
