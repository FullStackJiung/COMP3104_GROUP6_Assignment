DevOps and the Software Development Life Cycle

The Software Development Life Cycle, or SDLC, describes the stages used
to plan, design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain software.

DevOps supports the SDLC by connecting development work with testing,
deployment, operations, and continuous feedback.

Automation is one of the main benefits of DevOps.
Tasks such as builds, tests, and deployment checks can be executed
automatically through a CI/CD pipeline. This reduces repetitive manual work
and can detect problems earlier in the development process.
